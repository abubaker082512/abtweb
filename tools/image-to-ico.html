Why Multi-Resolution .ICO Files Matter for SEO & Windows

Google displays website favicons directly alongside search results on both mobile and desktop (requiring multiples of 48px like 48×48 or 192×192), while browser tabs use 16×16 or 32×32 and Windows desktop shortcuts use 256×256.

Simply renaming a `.png` to `.ico` breaks older browsers and Windows Explorer. Our binary encoder writes a genuine Microsoft ICO container with embedded PNG frames at every chosen size.
