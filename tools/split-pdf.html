Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visually see which pages I am extracting?
Yes! Every page of your PDF renders as a thumbnail card so you can click exactly the pages you want to keep.
How do page ranges work?
Type single pages or hyphenated ranges separated by commas—for example, 1-3, 5, 8-10—and press Enter to sync your visual selection.
Can I split every page into its own separate PDF file?
Yes! Select the pages you want (or click Select All) and click Split Each Page to ZIP to download them as individual PDFs inside one ZIP archive.
Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?
No. Both thumbnail rendering and PDF splitting run 100% locally inside your browser.
