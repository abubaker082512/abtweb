Frequently Asked Questions

Will the generated .PSD file open in Adobe Photoshop and Photopea?
Yes! It writes a standards-compliant 8-bit RGB/RGBA Photoshop binary (.psd) with both layer records and composite preview data.
Can I combine multiple PNGs into a single multi-layer PSD?
Yes! Drop multiple images, keep 'Pack All into 1 Multi-Layer PSD' selected, and every image becomes its own centered, named Photoshop layer.
Does PNG to PSD keep transparent backgrounds?
Yes. Unless you check 'Add White Background Layer', transparent pixels remain fully transparent on their respective layers.
Are my artwork files uploaded to a server?
Never. All PSD binary encoding runs directly inside your web browser.
