Frequently Asked Questions

How many PDF files can I merge at once?
You can merge as many PDFs as your browser's memory comfortably handles—there are no artificial file count caps.
Can I change the order of PDFs before merging?
Yes! Simply drag and drop any PDF card in the queue to reorder the sequence before clicking Merge.
Will merging reduce the quality of my PDFs?
No. Pages are copied natively without re-compressing text or vector graphics.
Are my confidential PDFs safe?
Yes. Everything runs strictly on your own device in JavaScript—no files are ever transmitted to ABT IT or any third party.
