Why Convert PNG to JPG?

JPEG compression was engineered specifically for photographic tones and gradients. When a photo or rich screenshot is stored as PNG, it wastes megabytes encoding tiny noise variations. Converting to JPG produces dramatically smaller files that upload faster and look identical to the human eye.

Unlike basic converters that turn transparent PNG regions pitch black, our tool lets you choose a clean white or custom hex background fill color before exporting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparent areas when converting PNG to JPG?
Since JPG does not support transparency, our tool fills transparent pixels with your chosen Background Fill Color (defaulting to crisp white #ffffff).
Will converting PNG to JPG reduce my file size?
Yes—typically by 60% to 85% for photographs and rich screenshots, shown live in our savings badge.
Is this PNG to JPG converter safe for confidential screenshots?
Completely safe. Conversion runs locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas, so nothing is ever transmitted over the internet.
What quality percentage should I use for JPG?
85% to 90% is ideal for high-clarity photos and product listings, while 70% to 80% maximizes speed for blog posts and emails.
